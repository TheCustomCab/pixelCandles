HomeKit slots

The five looks HomeKit can pick, by name. Tap one to show it. To put a look in a slot, use the Slot picker next to it on the Looks tab. Rename changes the names HomeKit shows.

Looks

A look is a flame behaviour plus one or more colours (palettes) alternating along the strings. Tap a look to show it and change it. Slot puts it in a HomeKit slot; Library keeps it in your library (looks without it live only in their slot).

LookSlotLibrary
Colours

Candle 1 takes colour 1, candle 2 colour 2, and so on along the string. Each colour is a palette: make and change palettes on the Palettes tab.

Flame

Every candle flickers on its own. Warm white LED lets the pixels' 3000 K white LED carry the pale, warm part of each colour; leave it on.

Made in WLED's palette maker

A multicolour look is showing: tapping a palette here turns every candle that one colour. Looks choose their colours on the Looks tab.

Tap a palette to try it on the candles. The flame picks its colour from the palette: the left end when it dips, the right end when it stands tall. Bars show the light as the LEDs give it (white LED included). Tap Edit to change a Candle palette's colours; looks that use it pick up the change. + New palette starts a copy of the palette that's showing for you to change. More palettes opens WLED's own palette maker.
